A platform built for one industry.

Brokerhaus is a private hiring network for Egyptian securities brokerage firms regulated by the FRA. We don't host CVs. We don't post jobs. We don't run a marketplace.

Instead, candidates describe the work they actually do — by department and function — and HR teams search for that work directly. Identity stays hidden until a candidate accepts a nomination.

Built in Cairo, for the brokerage industry, by people who know the industry.
