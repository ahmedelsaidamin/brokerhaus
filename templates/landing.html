// How it works

How the platform works for each side.

If you work in brokerage
Build · Wait · Accept
  1. Pick your department
    From 17 departments mapped to how brokerage firms actually operate.
  2. Check the functions you handle
    Tick everything you really do — even across multiple departments.
  3. Set seniority and your real job title
    Write the title from your contract — even if it's unusual.
  4. Set availability
    Available or Not Available. The second hides you completely.
  5. Receive nominations
    HR sends offers privately. Accept or decline. Your details stay hidden until you accept.
If you're hiring
Search · Filter · Invite
  1. Search by department
    Pick the department you're hiring for. The platform pre-filters relevant candidates.
  2. Tick required functions
    Match on work, not titles. "Settlements", "Margin calls", "Client onboarding".
  3. Filter seniority and experience
    Multi-select seniority. Set total years, and years per specific function.
  4. Review ranked candidates
    Match Score from 0–100. Identities hidden — only code, work, seniority, score.
  5. Select and nominate
    Send nominations to selected candidates. Each is valid for 30 days.

// Our rules

Six rules that protect your privacy.

RULE 01
No job applications.
There is no "Apply" button anywhere on the platform.
RULE 02
No job postings.
HR doesn't publish openings. They search and nominate directly.
RULE 03
Unavailable = invisible.
Marked "Not Available" means you disappear completely from search.
RULE 04
Contact details stay hidden.
No name, no phone, no email shown to HR until you accept.
RULE 05
Nominations expire in 30 days.
No backlog. Both sides move, or it closes quietly.
RULE 06
English by design.
One language. Functions and titles as the industry uses them.
// Questions

Things people actually ask.

Is the platform free for candidates?+
Yes. Always. Candidates never pay anything.
Will my current employer see my profile?+
No. Your identity is hidden behind a code (like C-1042). HR only sees your experience and work — name, phone, email, and current company stay private until you accept a nomination.
What if I'm happy in my current job?+
Set your status to 'Not Available'. You disappear completely from HR searches. Change it anytime.
How does match scoring work?+
A score out of 100: department match (20), function overlap (35), seniority (15), years of experience (15), function-specific years (10), availability (5).
Is this only for brokerage firms?+
Yes. Securities brokerage firms regulated by the FRA in Egypt only. The focus is what makes the platform work.
